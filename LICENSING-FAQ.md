# OttoFlow Licensing FAQ

OttoFlow is released under the [Business Source License 1.1](LICENSE.md)
(SPDX: `BUSL-1.1`) with an Additional Use Grant. This page explains, in plain
language, what that means in practice.

**The short version:** you can run OttoFlow in production, including for your
company's own business, for free. You cannot take OttoFlow and sell it — as a
product, as a hosted service, or embedded inside someone else's platform.

> This FAQ is interpretive guidance. Where it and [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md)
> disagree, the license controls. Questions:

## Using OttoFlow

**Can I run OttoFlow in production at my company?**
Yes. The Additional Use Grant explicitly permits production use, including use
in your internal business operations, on infrastructure you control. This
applies to for-profit companies, and to all affiliates under common control.

**Does "internal use" mean only internal-facing workloads?**
No. You can use OttoFlow to operate the clusters and workloads that run your
customer-facing business. The restriction is on selling OttoFlow itself, not on
what your business does.

**Can I modify OttoFlow?**
Yes — copy, modify, and create derivative works. Modified versions remain
subject to the same license, and you must keep the license displayed.

**Can I redistribute it?**
Yes, under this same license. Recipients get the same terms you did.

**Do I need to buy anything, or register?**
No. There is no license key, no telemetry gate, and no user or node cap.

**What happens after four years?**
Each version becomes available under the Apache License, Version 2.0, four
years after that version is published. See [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md) for the
per-version schedule. This is irrevocable.

## What is not permitted

**Can I offer OttoFlow as a hosted or managed service?**
No, not on a paid basis. Operating OttoFlow on behalf of a third party as a
paid service is a competitive offering. Contact
[hello@nirmata.com](mailto:hello@nirmata.com) for a commercial license.

**Can I embed OttoFlow in my product?**
Not if the product is a paid competitive offering. "Embedded" covers shipping
OttoFlow's code inside your product, and also packaging your product such that
OttoFlow must be downloaded or accessed for it to work.

**What counts as "competitive"?**
A paid product or service that significantly overlaps with the capabilities of
Nirmata's paid versions of OttoFlow, or with paid offerings Nirmata provides
for OttoFlow. If your offering is free, it is not competitive.

**Could my product become competitive later?**
Not because of us shipping features. If your product was not a competitive
offering when you first made it generally available, a later OttoFlow release
with additional capabilities does not retroactively make it one.

## Consultants, integrators, and service providers

**I'm an SI/consultant deploying OttoFlow for a client. Is that allowed?**
Yes, subject to three conditions:

1. OttoFlow runs in **infrastructure the client controls** — not yours.
2. You do **not** charge a license, subscription, or resale fee for OttoFlow
   itself. You may charge for your time and expertise.
3. You do **not** package OttoFlow as a productized or managed offering of your
   own.

Consulting, implementation, integration, migration, and training all fall
inside this safe harbor.

**Can I sell an ongoing paid support subscription for OttoFlow?**
No. Paid support arrangements for OttoFlow are within Nirmata's commercial
scope, and the professional-services safe harbor above does not cover them.
Partner arrangements are available — talk to us.

**Why is the line drawn at infrastructure and packaging?**
Because it separates labor from product. We are not trying to restrict who does
implementation work; enterprises should be able to bring in whichever partner
they want. We are protecting OttoFlow the product from being resold or rebadged.

## Contributing

**Do I need to sign a CLA?**
Yes. See [CLA.md](CLA.md) and [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). The CLA lets
Nirmata relicense contributions, which is what makes the guaranteed Apache-2.0
conversion possible.

**Is OttoFlow open source?**
No. BUSL-1.1 is a source-available license, not an OSI-approved open source
license. Each version becomes open source (Apache-2.0) on its Change Date.
